# Lumis Falcon 50 — Install Guide

Open the URL in your browser, then follow the instructions for your device.

## iPad / iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari** (must be Safari)
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with up-arrow at the bottom)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**
5. The Lumis Falcon 50 icon appears on your home screen — tap to launch
6. Works **offline** after first launch

## Mac (Sonoma 14 or newer)

1. Open the link in **Safari**
2. Click **File → Add to Dock**
3. Click the icon in your Dock to launch

Older macOS: open in Chrome or Edge → click the install icon in the address bar.

## Windows PC

1. Open in **Microsoft Edge** or **Chrome**
2. Click the **install icon** in the address bar
3. Click **Install**
4. App is added to Start Menu and Desktop
